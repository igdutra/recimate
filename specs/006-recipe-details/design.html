Petit Gâteau

Brazilian-style warm chocolate cake with a molten center, served with vanilla ice cream.

4 servings
1

Heat the oven to 220°C and butter four small ramekins.

2

Melt chocolate and butter together, then whisk in eggs, sugar, and flour.

3

Fill the ramekins and bake for 8 to 10 minutes, until the edges set and the centers stay soft.

4

Turn out while warm and serve immediately with vanilla ice cream.
