Design system

14 Design system: foundations

Colors, measured contrast, type, shape and spacing. Three colors plus white; everything else is ink at an opacity.

Colors

Three colors plus white. Everything else is ink at an opacity, so there is nothing else to name.

Green #2E7D4FActions, selected state, vegetarian mark, step numbers.SwiftUI: AccentColor (asset)
Ink #1B1F1DAll text and icons. Secondary text is ink at 66%.SwiftUI: Color("Ink") or .primary
Mist #EEF1EEFields, cards, grouped sections, image placeholders.SwiftUI: Color("Mist")
Secondary textInk 66%. Captions, hints, quantities.
SeparatorInk 12%. Row dividers.
Switch offInk 25%. Off track, sheet grabber.
PlaceholderInk 8%. No-image tile on a card.
ScrimInk 45%. Behind the sheet.

Contrast

Measured on the exact values above. Text needs 4.5:1, large text and icons 3:1.

15 Design system: components

15 pieces: ten native SwiftUI (tint only) and five custom views to write.

Components

Native means SwiftUI gives it for free, so only tint it and do not rebuild the drawing here. Custom means we write the view.

Primary buttonNative
Button with .borderedProminent, .buttonBorderShape(.capsule), tint set to the accent. 44 in states, 52 in the sheet.
Secondary and text buttonNative
.bordered and .borderless button styles with the accent tint.
Filter chipCustom
A Toggle with a custom ToggleStyle (capsule, mist off, green on). Servings is a Menu with the same label style.
Search fieldNative
.searchable(text:). iOS 26 draws its own bar, so this is a stand-in: do not rebuild it.
Recipe cardCustom
Lemon Herb Chicken
4 servings
RecipeCardView(recipe:). Mist background, 20 radius, photo on top, title and servings below. Takes a RecipePreview.
Image placeholderCustom
The AsyncImage .empty and .failure phases draw this; so does a nil image URL. Used in the card and the detail hero.
Vegetarian mark and badgeCustom
Vegetarian
A leaf (SF Symbol "leaf") on cards, a filled badge in Detail. Filled because green text on mist is 4.4:1.
Segmented controlNative
Picker with .pickerStyle(.segmented). Servings (1–2, 3–4, 5+) and, if chosen, the Ingredients / Steps tabs. iOS 26 renders it as glass.
Switch rowNative
Vegetarian
Toggle("Vegetarian", isOn:) inside a Form section, .tint(accent).
Disclosure rowNative
NavigationLink or a Button with a chevron in a Form. Entry behind it is backlog.
