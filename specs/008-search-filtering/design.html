ReciMate search and filters

Read-only snapshot for spec 008, adapted from the spec 003 design with the discovery decisions applied: no quick filter chips, one Filters button in the toolbar, the search field searches instruction text, the Filters sheet applies live (Done and Reset, no Apply button), servings is an exact count, include and exclude ingredients are free-text terms, and the no-results state is the system ContentUnavailableView.search. Static: nothing animates and nothing is clickable. The system draws the search field and toolbar controls on iOS 26 as glass, so the SwiftUI will look different there and should not be rebuilt to match. Error, loading and empty-collection states are not part of this spec.
